Release Notes

v0.4.0 September 4, 2026

Nearby places, conscious saving and offline use.

  • Places around you — After fetching your position, locate.me suggests nearby places — cafés, restaurants, shops, healthcare, leisure and more — sorted by distance. Tap one and you save exactly that place, with its name, address and icon.
  • Choose what you save — Before saving you decide: keep your GPS position with its resolved address, or switch to one of the suggested places. Go back any time — nothing is stored until you confirm.
  • Offline use — After a single online visit, locate.me opens even without a connection: the app starts and your recent history stays readable. A small note tells you when you're looking at cached data.
  • History list & map stay in sync — The search filter now applies to the map as well, and the map pins carry the same numbers as the list — so switching views never loses your orientation.
  • More robust fetching — If the places service is temporarily slow or unreachable, you still get your address and weather right away and can save as usual — with a clear message instead of a long wait.
  • Fixes & improvements — Stability and protection improvements behind the scenes keep locate.me running smoothly.
v0.3.0 August 17, 2026

Richer places, better travel planning and a brand-new homepage.

  • UV index & elevation — Every fetched location now shows the UV index and elevation — right when you fetch it, and again later in your history, so you can plan the day before you head out.
  • Tags & comments — When saving, add a tag from a ready-made list (e.g. Home, Work, Parking) and a short comment (up to 25 characters). Both optional; they appear in your history and on the saved-location card.
  • Biking & driving times — History now shows not just walking time, but also how long you'd take by bike or car — together with the distance.
  • Demo mode banner — A clear banner appears whenever the shared demo user ID is active, so you always know you're in demo mode.
  • Request your own user ID — From the settings you can now request your own private user ID via email — no more sharing the demo account.
  • New website — locate.me has a brand-new bilingual homepage at locate-me.net, with release notes and an open-source link.
  • Fixes & improvements — Saving a location no longer fails in some cases, history cards are cleaner, and the app feels more responsive.
v0.2.3 July 26, 2026

A modern design, faster interaction and handy new features for your location history.

  • Dark mode — Easy on your eyes at night. Switch it in the settings; your choice is remembered and the app starts in the matching look.
  • Share places right from the map — Tap a pin in your history map view and the info popup now includes its own share button.
  • Quick search in your history — A new search field above the list filters your entries in real time as you type an address or city.
  • Pull-to-refresh — Pull the history down briefly to reload the entries; a visual arrow tells you when to let go.
  • Smoother loading feel — Elegant placeholder cards appear while your history loads, for a noticeably more fluid app.
v0.2.1 July 16, 2026

Capturing and sharing your places is now even easier and more intuitive.

  • Live counter in the menu — A badge on the History icon shows at a glance how many places you have saved.
  • Share places directly — The new “Share” button sends your locations instantly through your phone's familiar share menu (e.g. WhatsApp).
  • OpenStreetMap integration — Your places are now visualized on real maps — both in search and in history.
  • Clean numbering — No more cryptic database IDs. Your history is simply and clearly numbered.
  • Tappable status dot — Tap the pulsing dot at the top right to see at once whether your app is online.
  • System info — The settings now show all important background data about your app version. Your user ID is no longer displayed in plain text.
v0.2.0 July 2, 2026

The first milestone of locate.me is here.

  • Preview before saving — See your position before you store it.
  • Category icons — Saved places are marked with a category-specific icon.
  • Distance & walking time — See the distance and walking time from your current location to a saved place.
